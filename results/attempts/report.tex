\section*{Table 3 — Behavior policy fit quality per round (train split)}

{\small Truncated Gaussian fitted per round via L-BFGS-B ($\mu$ degree 1, $\log\sigma$ degree 2).
RMSE and MAE measure the deviation of predicted difficulty $\hat\delta(\theta)$ from observed $\delta$.
\colorbox{red!20}{Red rows} were auto-excluded (RMSE $> 5.0$): the optimizer found a degenerate

difficulty distribution with a spike at $\delta_{\min}$.
